# Product Guidelines

## Communication & Tone
- **Helpful & Encouraging:** The platform should act like a knowledgeable gardening mentor, providing supportive feedback and clear guidance.
- **Technical & Precise:** For research and data analysis, the tone must be accurate and clinical to maintain professional trust.
- **Simple & Direct:** Instructions must be concise and actionable, ensuring the mobile app is easy to use while working in the field.

## Visual Identity & Design Principles
- **Clean & Modern:** Prioritize a high-whitespace layout with minimalist components to ensure information clarity and a professional aesthetic.
- **Professionalism:** Use a refined color palette and consistent typography to distinguish between administrative, research, and field-use interfaces.
- **Clarity over Complexity:** Design UI elements to be intuitive, reducing cognitive load for users who may be multi-tasking in an agricultural environment.
